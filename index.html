Calculator

A free online calculator with three keypads: standard, scientific and programmer. It runs on exact decimal arithmetic, so 0.1 + 0.2 really is 0.3, keeps a running history of your recent calculations above the display, shows HEX, DEC, OCT and BIN side by side with the bitwise operators, supports the full keyboard, works offline and installs to the home screen.
